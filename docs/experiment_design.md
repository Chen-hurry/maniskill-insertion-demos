# Experiment Design

## Tasks

- Pick and place.
- Object sorting.
- Drawer opening.

## Metrics

- Task success rate.
- Anomaly detection precision and recall.
- Recovery success rate after anomaly onset.
- Extra recovery steps and wall-clock intervention cost.
- Failure mode breakdown by anomaly type.

## Comparisons

- Nominal policy without recovery.
- Rule-based recovery.
- Critic-triggered learned recovery.
- VLM/VLA recovery.
- Hybrid recovery.
